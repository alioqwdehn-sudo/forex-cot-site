Methodology and official source

All markets use CFTC Legacy Futures Only and the Non-Commercial category exclusively. Net = Long minus Short. Weekly change = current net minus previous net. Bullish means net increased from the first to the last of six reports; Bearish means it decreased; Neutral means flat or fewer than six observations. This describes positioning momentum, not an entry signal or validated price forecast. The COT Index measures net within its 52-report range; a flat range returns 50. Report dates refer to positions, not publication. Spreading is excluded from directional net.
